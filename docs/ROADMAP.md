# Delivery roadmap

| Milestone | Indicative duration | Exit criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M0 — Discovery & prototype | Weeks 1–3 | Tested camera setup and static chord prototype; consent and positioning validated with 8–12 learners |
| M1 — Technical foundation | Weeks 4–7 | iPhone/iPad app shell, offline curriculum, on-device pipeline, privacy baseline, CI |
| M2 — Guided chord MVP | Weeks 8–13 | C/G/D/Em/Am static-shape assessment, setup guidance, ranked feedback, local progress, accessibility pass |
| M3 — Beta quality | Weeks 14–18 | TestFlight cohort, measured accuracy/latency, left-handed support, Sign in with Apple and private sync, crash/performance hardening |
| M4 — App Store launch | Weeks 19–22 | Store listing, paywall experiment, support/privacy operations, launch campaign, production monitoring |
| M5 — Growth learning loops | Post-launch | Teacher/parent summaries, retention experiments, broader exercise library, evaluated model iteration |

## Phase gates

- Do not build audio scoring or dynamic strumming until static-shape feedback reaches the agreed trust threshold in beta.
- Do not collect diagnostic video until the value, consent wording, retention, and deletion workflow pass privacy review.
- Do not launch a subscription until activation and week-one practice behaviour demonstrate repeated value.

## Launch funnel

Acquisition → App Store page → first open → camera setup complete → first chord assessed → first successful retry → week-1 practice streak → paid path/teacher plan. Instrument each transition with a consent-aware, aggregate-safe event.
